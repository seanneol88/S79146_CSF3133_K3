Destinations

Three places our student guides recommend for a weekend away. Prices are per person and include the boat or bus transfer from Kuala Terengganu.

Pulau Redang

White sand, water clear enough to see your own feet, and a marine park full of coral. The snorkelling trip leaves at nine in the morning.

RM 399 RM 299

Masjid Kristal

The Crystal Mosque on Wan Man Island, built from steel, glass and crystal. Free to enter outside prayer times, and best photographed at sunset.

RM 150 RM 99

Kampung Cina

Chinatown in the middle of Kuala Terengganu: painted shophouses, a coffee shop on every corner and the weekend night market.

RM 220 RM 159
