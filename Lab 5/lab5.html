Robotics club takes second place

Six students from the faculty spent eleven weeks building a line-following robot and came home with the runner-up trophy from the state competition.

The chassis is laser-cut acrylic, the brain is a single-board computer, and the line sensor was tuned by hand on the cafeteria floor at two in the morning.

New lab benches for the second floor

Twenty-four workstations were installed over the semester break. Each bench now has its own power strip and a monitor arm, so nobody has to fight over the socket by the door.

Why the library extended its hours

From this week the library closes at eleven instead of nine, Monday to Thursday, after a survey showed that most final-year students study after dinner.
