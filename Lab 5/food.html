About the Festival

Three days of cooking, tasting and arguing about whose keropok lekor is the better one. Forty stalls will set up along Dataran Shahbandar, from the fishermen of Kuala Terengganu to the students who have spent the semester perfecting a single recipe.

The festival opens at ten each morning and stays lit until eleven at night. Cooking demonstrations run on the main stage at two and at seven, and the children's corner keeps the youngest visitors busy while the queue for satar slowly shortens. Entry is free, everything is halal, and the whole site is a short walk from the Shahbandar jetty.

Free entry for everyone, all three days.

Finding Us

Dataran Shahbandar sits on the river mouth, ten minutes on foot from Pasar Payang. Parking is available behind the council office, and the shuttle from the bus station runs every twenty minutes between nine in the morning and midnight.
