Terengganu, once written Trengganu or Tringganu, is a sultanate and a constituent state of Malaysia, also known by the Arabic honorific Darul Iman, "Abode of Faith". Kuala Terengganu, the coastal capital, stands at the mouth of the broad Terengganu River and is both the royal town and the largest city in the state. Several islands lie close to the coast, among them the Perhentian Islands and Redang Island.
