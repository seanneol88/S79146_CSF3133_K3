Nothing in the markup of the four cards changes. Each card is flex: 1 1 22% by default; the 900px query raises the basis to 44% so only two fit on a line, and the 600px query raises it to 100% so each one takes a full row.

What a media query is made of

  • @media — the at-rule that starts it.
  • screen — the media type; print is the other common one.
  • and (max-width: 600px) — the condition that has to be true.
  • The braces hold ordinary rules, exactly as they would elsewhere.

The <meta name="viewport"> tag in the head is what makes these numbers mean real device pixels on a phone. Without it the phone pretends to be 980 pixels wide and every breakpoint is skipped.
